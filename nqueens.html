How to Use N-Queens
An interactive backtracking visualizer — place N non-attacking queens on an N×N board.
Getting Started
1
Select a Board Size (N) from the dropdown — 4×4 is great for learning, 8×8 is the classic challenge.
2
Adjust the Speed slider — drag left for slow motion to follow each step, right for a fast overview.
3
Click Visualize! to run the full backtracking animation automatically.
Reading the Board
4
Golden crown = a Queen currently placed. Red overlay = cells attacked by a queen. Blue pulse = the cell being tried.
5
When all queens are safely placed, the board glows green and a mini-board is added to the Solutions Gallery on the right.
6
Watch the Algorithm Tracker on the right panel — the highlighted line shows exactly which line of pseudocode is executing.
Step Mode
7
Click Step to advance one step at a time — great for studying how backtracking works in detail.
8
Click any mini-board in the Solutions Gallery to jump directly to that solution and see it on the main board.
